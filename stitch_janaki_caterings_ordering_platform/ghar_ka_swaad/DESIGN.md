## Brand & Style

The brand personality centers on the purity, warmth, and nostalgia of authentic Indian home cooking (*Ghar Jaisa Khana*). It evokes the reassurance of maternal care, culinary honesty, and kitchen freshness. The target audience includes busy urban professionals, students living away from home, and families seeking clean, wholesome daily meal subscriptions and reliable lunch/dinner deliveries without commercial grease or heavy restaurant spices.

The visual style leans into **Warm Contemporary Organic Minimalism**:
- Pristine, sunlit kitchen aesthetics using soft warm creams instead of sterile whites.
- Deep forest greens that signal farm-fresh vegetables, cleanliness, and health.
- Warm saffron and spice-toned accents that highlight rich gravies, slow-cooked dal, and seasonal thalis without cluttering the screen.
- Generous, rounded physical cards mimicking traditional service trays (*thalis*) and clean kitchen prep surfaces.
- Tactile, reassuring micro-interactions and explicit culinary badges (such as the traditional green-dot vegetarian marker and subscription combo tags) that provide instant clarity and trust.

## Colors

The palette establishes an appetizing, comforting kitchen atmosphere with immediate trust signals:

- **Primary (`#38754A`)**: Deep Forest Green. Represents nourishment, authenticity, and natural freshness. Used for primary CTA buttons, selected subscription states, active navigation tabs, and brand markers.
  - Hover / Interactive Dark: `#285C38`
- **Secondary / Accent (`#E99A32`)**: Warm Spice / Saffron Orange. Evokes turmeric, roasted cumin, and saffron. Reserved for combo highlights, meal badges, discount ribbons, rating stars, and floating cart alerts.
- **Tertiary / Success (`#287A49`)**: Verdant Basil Green. Dedicated to the universal Indian vegetarian indicator, successful checkout validations, and "Delivered Fresh" states.
- **Neutral Primary (`#292D26`)**: Deep Charcoal Olive. Provides high-contrast, organic legibility for body copy and headings without the harsh sterility of true black.
- **Neutral Muted (`#77786F`)**: Warm Earth Gray. Serves meta labels, nutritional details, ingredient lists, and delivery estimates.
- **Canvas / Page Background (`#FFF9F0`)**: Warm Cream. Evokes steamed basmati rice and wholesome dough, establishing warmth across negative space.
- **Surface / Card Background (`#FFFFFF`)**: Pure White. Creates crisp, elevated focal planes for meal cards, modal sheets, and receipt breakdowns.
- **Borders (`#EEE7DC`)**: Soft Biscuit Line. Delicately separates meal cards, dividers, and ingredient tiers without introducing dark, industrial visual clutter.
- **Destructive / Error (`#C43D36`)**: Kashmiri Chilli Red. For stockouts, delivery alerts, and form validation errors.

## Typography

The type system blends the grounded, friendly geometry of **DM Sans** for all editorial headlines, meal names, and pricing with the neutral, hyper-legible utility of **Inter** for descriptions, nutritional badges, customization toggles, and checkout tables.

- **Headlines & Dishes**: Set in DM Sans with tight letter-spacing to make meal titles (e.g., "North Indian Executive Thali", "Dal Tadka with Phulkas") feel appetizing and substantial.
- **Body & Editorial Copy**: Inter at regular weight provides effortless reading across phone screens, particularly when listing daily changing tiffin menus, ingredients, and delivery instructions.
- **Badges & Numbers**: Labels, prices, and quantities are set with medium-to-semibold weights in Inter to ensure immediate scanning during fast mobile checkout.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around a mobile-first, vertical rhythm tailored for on-the-go lunchtime ordering and recurring subscription scheduling:

- **Mobile Viewports (< 768px)**:
  - Outer margins: `16px` (`1rem`).
  - Single-column flow with continuous vertical stack for meal cards, horizontal swipe ribbons for daily menu calendar tabs (Mon - Sun), and a sticky floating bottom bar for order totals.
  - Card gutters: `16px` (`1rem`).
- **Tablet / Small Desktop (768px - 1024px)**:
  - 8-column layout with `24px` margins and `16px` gutters.
  - Meal cards organize into a balanced 2-column grid.
- **Desktop (1024px+)**:
  - Max container width constrained to `1200px` centered within `#FFF9F0`.
  - 12-column grid with `40px` (`2.5rem`) outer canvas margins and `24px` (`1.5rem`) gutters.
  - Split view architecture: 8 columns dedicated to the meal/thali selection and weekly calendar; 4 columns dedicated to an anchored, real-time tiffin schedule and checkout overview.
- **Component Padding Scale**:
  - `space-xs` (4px): Badge padding, icon-text gap.
  - `space-sm` (8px): Button internal vertical padding, tag margins.
  - `space-md` (16px): Card internal content padding, list item vertical spacing.
  - `space-lg` (24px): Card group breaks, section separation within modals.
  - `space-xl` (32px): Primary section breaks (e.g., Breakfast vs. Lunch vs. Subscription Combos).

## Elevation & Depth

To preserve an airy, domestic, and non-commercial look, depth is achieved via soft ambient drop shadows tinted with warm earthen undertones rather than cold grays, paired with subtle borders.

- **Level 0 (Flat / Canvas)**: `#FFF9F0` page background.
- **Level 1 (Resting Cards & Tiles)**: Background `#FFFFFF` with a 1px border of `#EEE7DC` and an ambient shadow: `0px 2px 8px -1px rgba(41, 45, 38, 0.05), 0px 1px 3px 0px rgba(41, 45, 38, 0.03)`.
- **Level 2 (Active Meal Card / Hover States)**: `0px 8px 20px -3px rgba(41, 45, 38, 0.08), 0px 3px 6px -1px rgba(41, 45, 38, 0.04)`, border shifting to `#38754A` at 20% opacity.
- **Level 3 (Sticky Bottom Bar & Drawers)**: Pure white surface with top border `1px solid #EEE7DC` and upward shadow: `0px -4px 16px rgba(41, 45, 38, 0.06)`.
- **Level 4 (Dialogs & Customization Modals)**: Centered or bottom-sheet overlays with backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(4px)`) over an ambient overlay `rgba(41, 45, 38, 0.4)` and card shadow `0px 16px 36px -4px rgba(41, 45, 38, 0.16)`.

## Shapes

The design uses a dedicated **14px corner radius** for all core product cards, conveying friendliness, handcrafted care, and comfort.

- **Primary Cards & Containers**: `14px` (`0.875rem`). This exact radius softens meal imagery, thali cards, and billing summaries.
- **Buttons & Pill Badges**: Fully rounded pills (`9999px`) for quick meal tags (e.g., "Pure Desi Ghee", "Low Oil"), category selector tabs, and quantity stepper controls.
- **Input Fields & Quantity Steppers**: `10px` to maintain a unified gentle structure without looking overly pill-shaped.
- **Vegetarian Symbol**: A precise `16px` outer square with `2px` rounded corners, housing a centered `8px` solid circular pip, adhering strictly to authentic food packaging identity.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Add to Cart / Confirm Subscription)**:
  - Background `#38754A`, text `#FFFFFF`, font `Inter` semibold 14px.
  - Height: `44px` on mobile, `48px` on desktop. Border radius: `10px` or full pill (`9999px`) based on context.
  - Hover: Background `#285C38`.
  - Active: Scale transform `0.98`.
- **Secondary / Ghost Button (Customize / View Thali Details)**:
  - Background transparent, border `1px solid #38754A`, text `#38754A`.
  - Hover: Background `rgba(56, 117, 74, 0.08)`.
- **Accent Action (Special Offers / Combos)**:
  - Background `#E99A32`, text `#FFFFFF`.
  - Hover: Background `#D18423`.

### Food & Diet Badges
- **Pure Veg Indicator**:
  - `16px` square border in `#287A49` (1.5px stroke width, 2px radius) enclosing a centered solid circle of `#287A49` (diameter 8px).
- **Spice Level / Special Tag (e.g., "Ghar Style", "No Onion Garlic")**:
  - Background `#FFF9F0`, border `1px solid #EEE7DC`, text `#77786F`, font size `11px`, weight 600, padding `3px 8px`, pill radius.
- **Combo & Chef's Special Badge**:
  - Background `#E99A32` at 12% tint, text `#B86D12`, font size `11px`, weight 700, padding `4px 10px`, pill radius.

### Meal & Thali Cards
- Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #EEE7DC`, radius `14px`, overflow hidden.
- Top section: 16:9 ratio crisp imagery of the meal with optional top-left absolute badge ("Today's Lunch Special").
- Content padding `16px`. Title in `DM Sans` 18px semibold (`#292D26`). Subtitle/items in `Inter` 13px (`#77786F`) showing the breakdown (e.g., *2 Phulkas, Paneer Bhurji, Yellow Dal Tadka, Jeera Rice, Salad*).
- Bottom row: Price in `DM Sans` 18px bold (`#292D26`) paired with an integrated `+ ADD` or numerical quantity stepper (`- 1 +`) button in primary green.

### Quantity Stepper
- Compact pill container (`#38754A` border or solid `#38754A` background).
- Decrement, count, and increment arranged horizontally with ample touch area (minimum touch target 36x36px).
- White text if filled, green text if outlined.

### Input Fields & Address Selectors
- Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1.5px solid #EEE7DC`, radius `10px`, padding `12px 14px`. Text `#292D26`.
- Focus state: Border `#38754A`, subtle ring `0 0 0 3px rgba(56, 117, 74, 0.15)`.
- Helper text in `#77786F` (12px).

### Weekly Meal Subscription Calendar Bar
- Horizontal scrolling pill strip for days of the week (Mon to Sun).
- Unselected: White background, `#EEE7DC` border, `#77786F` day/date text.
- Selected: `#38754A` solid background, `#FFFFFF` text, indicating active meal selection day.

### Sticky Checkout Drawer (Mobile)
- Docked to screen bottom with upward ambient shadow. Pure white background.
- Left column: Total price with itemized count ("2 Meals • ₹240").
- Right column: "View Cart" primary green button with a chevron icon.
